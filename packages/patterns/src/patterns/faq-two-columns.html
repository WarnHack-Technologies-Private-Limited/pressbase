Questions

Before you get in touch

Quick answers to common questions. Open one to read the answer.

How do I book?

Use the contact page, call us or drop in. We will confirm a time that suits you.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what you need. We always agree the price with you before we start, so there are no surprises.

How soon can you start?

As soon as we can. Tell us when suits you and we will confirm a date.

What should I bring?

Just yourself and any questions. If we need anything else, we will tell you when you book.

What if I need to change my booking?

Let us know as early as you can and we will find another time together.

Where can I find you?

Our address and a map are on the contact page.
